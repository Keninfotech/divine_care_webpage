Home Overview

Heritage charm meets enhanced specialist care

Set within the peaceful residential conservation area of Blairhill, Coatbridge, Deanston Enhanced Care combines the architectural grace of a historic Scottish manor house with purpose-renovated modern clinical facilities.

Registered for up to 32 residents, Deanston provides an intimate, family-scale care environment where every individual receives dedicated, unhurried attention from our nursing and care team. Located just minutes from the M8 and within easy walking distance of Blairhill railway station, it is ideally situated for family visits from Glasgow, North Lanarkshire and across Central Scotland.

Care We Provide

Tailored clinical excellence for 32 residents

At Deanston Enhanced Care, our care pathways are tailored around detailed personal assessments, providing continuous 24-hour nursing and care support across both general and complex conditions.

Care Pathways

7 Care Categories Provided

Category 01

Nursing Care

24-hour registered nursing cover managing complex healthcare needs, clinical medication, wound dressings and ongoing vital monitoring.

Category 02

Dementia Care

Specialist, person-centred dementia support designed to reassure, orientate, and maintain familiar routines and personal dignity.

Category 03

Frail Elderly & Residential

Assistance with washing, dressing, mobility, dining and day-to-day living within a reassuringly secure, warm home.

Category 04

Palliative & End-of-Life Care

Compassionate, dignified clinical care and symptom control planned in close liaison with families, GPs and Macmillan palliative teams.

Category 05

Respite & Short-Term Stays

Temporary placement offering recovery after hospital discharge, short-term support, or valuable respite for family carers.

Category 06

Mental Health Care

Supportive environment for residents living with age-related mental health conditions, depression, anxiety and cognitive changes.

Category 07

Physical Disability Support

Adaptive care, specialized equipment, assisted bathing, and tailored mobility plans to maintain independence and comfort.

Living Environment

Facilities designed for dignity & wellbeing

Deanston’s historic architecture has been thoughtfully repurposed into five core facility groupings designed to foster community, safety and comfort.

Group 1

Single En-Suite Bedrooms Over Two Floors

Private, light-filled rooms with full en-suite facilities, specialist profiling beds, call bells, TV points, and space for cherished personal keepsakes.

Group 2

Sensory & Relaxation Room

A serene space equipped with multi-sensory bubble tubes, fibre-optic sprays, relaxing acoustic music, and tactile aids for calming reflection.

Group 3

Indoor Garden & Grounds

An indoor heated garden providing nature in all Scottish weather, alongside landscaped, secure outdoor gardens with gentle walking paths.

Group 4

VR Immersive Experience Lounge

A modern digital relaxation lounge featuring safe, supervised virtual reality equipment for memory stimulation and travel experiences.

Group 5

Dining Rooms & Lounges

A communal dining room serving fresh home cooking, quiet library corners, visitor lounges, and dedicated assisted bathing suites.
