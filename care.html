Expert Nursing Care with Compassion at Its Heart

Specialist Memory Support

Understanding Dementia. Supporting People. Valuing Every Individual.

Living with dementia can bring challenges for individuals and their families. At Divine Care Homes Scotland Ltd., we believe that every person living with dementia deserves understanding, patience, respect and meaningful support. Our approach recognises the person behind the diagnosis.

"We see the person, not just the condition."

Person-Centred Dementia Care Focus:

  • Understanding each person's unique life history and identity
  • Maintaining reassuring, familiar routines wherever possible
  • Promoting independence and personal choice in everyday living
  • Providing emotional support and communicating in ways that work
  • Encouraging meaningful activities and social interactions
  • Specialist sensory therapy and VR experiences at Deanston Enhanced Care

Short Stays & Convalescence

A Short Stay. The Same Compassionate Care.

Caring for a loved one is rewarding but demanding. Respite care provides an opportunity for individuals to receive temporary care and support in a welcoming environment, while family members take time to rest, attend to commitments or recharge.

"Because caring for carers matters too."

Who Can Benefit from Respite Care?

  • Individuals whose family carers require a well-deserved break
  • Individuals recovering and rebuilding confidence after hospital admission
  • People needing temporary additional assistance or convalescence
  • Families considering longer-term care wishing to trial a short stay
