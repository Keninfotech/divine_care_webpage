Professional Clinical Oversight

Expert nursing care with compassion at its heart

At Divine Care Homes Scotland Ltd., we understand that individuals requiring nursing care may have complex or changing health needs. Our aim is to provide professional nursing support within a caring, comfortable and reassuring environment where residents are treated with dignity and respect. We believe that clinical excellence and compassionate care should always go hand in hand.

"Professional care. Personal attention. A DIVINE touch."

Nursing support may include:

  • Ongoing nursing assessment and monitoring
  • Medication management and administration
  • Support with long-term health conditions
  • Wound care and clinical interventions within staff competence
  • Continence care and personal hygiene
  • Nutritional support and monitoring
  • Mobility assistance and falls prevention
  • Liaison with GPs, community nurses and healthcare professionals
  • End-of-life and palliative support, where appropriate

Promoting Comfort and Quality of Life

We recognise that nursing care is about much more than clinical tasks. We aim to maintain residents' independence wherever possible, encourage meaningful activities and support their emotional and social wellbeing.

Specialist Memory Support

Understanding Dementia. Supporting People. Valuing Every Individual.

Living with dementia can bring challenges for individuals and their families. At Divine Care Homes Scotland Ltd., we believe that every person living with dementia deserves understanding, patience, respect and meaningful support. Our approach recognises the person behind the diagnosis. We understand that dementia affects individuals differently. Their experiences, abilities, interests and support needs are unique.

"We see the person, not just the condition."

Person-Centred Dementia Care:

  • Understanding each person's life history and identity
  • Maintaining familiar routines wherever possible
  • Promoting independence and personal choice
  • Providing reassurance and emotional support
  • Supporting communication in ways that work for each individual
  • Encouraging meaningful activities and social interaction
  • Maintaining dignity, privacy and respect
  • Working closely with families and representatives

A Supportive Environment & Supporting Families

We recognise the importance of a calm, welcoming and familiar environment for people living with dementia. We aim to reduce unnecessary confusion, promote orientation and encourage activities that reflect residents' abilities. Dementia affects the whole family, and we warmly encourage relatives to remain involved in their loved one's life and care.

Homely Community Living

A Place to Feel at Home

At Divine Care Homes Scotland Ltd., we believe that moving into residential care should be the beginning of a new chapter, not the end of independence. Our residential care approach is designed to support individuals who require assistance with everyday activities but do not necessarily need continuous nursing care. We aim to provide a safe, comfortable and welcoming home where residents can enjoy companionship, choice and independence.

"A home where you are welcomed, respected and cared for."

Personalised Daily Support:

  • Assistance with washing, dressing and personal hygiene
  • Support with mobility and everyday activities
  • Medication support in accordance with individual care plans
  • Nutritious meals, refreshments and hydration support
  • Full laundry and domestic housekeeping
  • Social activities, outings and recreational opportunities
  • Emotional, physical and practical daily support

Maintaining Independence

We encourage residents to maintain their abilities, interests and daily routines for as long as possible. Whether it is enjoying a favourite hobby, spending time with friends, or simply relaxing in a comfortable environment, we believe that every day should offer opportunities for enjoyment and fulfilment.

Short-Term & Convalescent Stays

A Short Stay. The Same Compassionate Care.

At Divine Care Homes Scotland Ltd., we understand that caring for a loved one can be both rewarding and demanding. Respite care provides an opportunity for individuals to receive temporary care and support in a welcoming environment, while family members and unpaid carers take time to rest, attend to personal commitments or simply recharge.

"Because caring for carers matters too."

Who Can Benefit from Respite Care?

  • Individuals whose family carers require a temporary break
  • People recovering after a hospital stay, where a short-term placement is appropriate
  • Individuals who need temporary additional support
  • Families seeking short-term care arrangements
  • Families considering longer-term care wishing to explore a short stay

What Can Residents Expect?

During a respite stay, residents receive support based on their individual assessed needs: personal care, daily living assistance, nursing support where clinically required, medication support, nutritious meals, companionship, and full inclusion in social activities.

About Divine Care Homes Scotland Ltd.

Welcoming communities where residents feel they belong
