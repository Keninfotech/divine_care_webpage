Home Overview

Modern, purpose-built comfort in the heart of Whifflet

Centenary House Care Home has been purpose-built from the ground up to provide a safe, accessible and deeply welcoming home. Every aspect of the building—from step-free level thresholds and wide corridors to abundant natural light and secure sensory outdoor courtyards—is designed to support resident comfort and mobility.

Registered for up to 30 residents, our smaller, attentive scale ensures our nursing and care teams know each resident as an individual. Positioned right in Whifflet, Coatbridge, our residents remain connected to the local Scottish community, with nearby shops, bus routes and Whifflet railway station just moments away.

Care We Provide

Comprehensive care for up to 30 residents

At Centenary House Care Home, our professional nursing and care teams deliver three core pathways designed around dignity, safety and individual wellbeing.

Pathway 01

Nursing Care

24-hour qualified nursing oversight providing skilled clinical support for individuals with ongoing, complex, or changing healthcare needs.

  • Continuous clinical monitoring & assessments
  • Accurate medication administration & reviews
  • Wound management, dressing changes & pressure relief
  • Falls prevention, mobility support & physiotherapy liaisons
  • Multidisciplinary coordination with Lanarkshire NHS & GPs

Pathway 02

Residential Care

Safe, comfortable, and dignified support for residents who require day-to-day assistance but do not need continuous clinical nursing interventions.

  • Gentle assistance with dressing, washing & grooming
  • Wholesome dining, hydration & dietary planning
  • Full laundry, housekeeping and room servicing
  • Engaging daily social activities, games & entertainment
  • Encouraging autonomy and personal independence

Smooth Transitions

Settling in: A warm, reassuring journey

Moving into a care home is a major life transition. At Centenary House, our settling-in framework is designed to make the journey as comforting, positive and stress-free as possible for residents and their families.

01

A Warm Welcome

From the moment you step through our doors, you are greeted with genuine Scottish warmth. We prepare your room ahead of time with your preferred arrangement, welcome you with a fresh cup of tea or favourite beverage, and introduce you gently to your carers and neighbours at a pace that feels comfortable to you.

02

Your Personal Key Worker

Every resident at Centenary House is assigned a dedicated Key Worker. Your key worker acts as your primary carer, personal advocate and trusted friend. They ensure your daily preferences are championed, keep your room feeling homely, and serve as the direct, familiar contact point for your family.

03

Getting to Know You

Good care starts with knowing the person behind the name. We spend time learning your life story—your hobbies, favourite meals, musical tastes, personal routines, and family milestones. This personal knowledge shapes your care plan so every day feels meaningful and familiar.

"Our promise is simple: we will take the time to listen, learn and welcome you with open arms until Centenary House truly feels like home."
Features & Amenities

Purpose-built amenities for your ease & comfort

Centenary House has been crafted specifically for accessible, modern living in Coatbridge.

Feature 1

Private En-Suite Accommodations

Spacious single bedrooms with private en-suite shower rooms, profiling beds, nurse call buttons, and room for family photographs and furniture.

Feature 2

Level-Access Throughout

Modern step-free design with passenger lift access to all areas, wide dementia-friendly hallways, and secure handrails for confident mobility.

Feature 3

Bright Dining Room & Lounges

Sunlit communal dining space serving fresh Scottish seasonal menus, alongside cosy quiet sitting rooms for visiting family and reading.

Feature 4

Enclosed Courtyard Garden

Safe, sheltered outdoor courtyard patio with raised planters, outdoor seating and level paths for enjoying fresh air and sunshine safely.

Feature 5

Salon & Visiting Healthcare

Dedicated on-site hair styling and pampering salon, plus regular visits from NHS community doctors, podiatrists, opticians and dentists.
