Vacancies & Roles

Career Opportunities

Depending on current vacancies across Deanston Enhanced Care and Centenary House Care Home, our opportunities include:

Clinical

Registered Nurses (RGN / RMN)

Provide professional nursing care, clinical leadership and support to residents and care teams across day and night shifts.

Care Team

Senior Care Assistants

Support the delivery of high-quality care, assist with team coordination, medication management and promote good practice.

Care Team

Care Assistants

Support residents with personal care, daily living, companionship, recreation and meaningful social activities.

Hospitality

Housekeeping & Domestic Staff

Help maintain an impeccably clean, comfortable, sanitary and welcoming home environment for all residents and visitors.

Hospitality

Kitchen & Catering Staff

Contribute to providing delicious, nutritious home-cooked meals, dietary accommodations and a positive dining experience.

Operations

Management & Administration

Support effective leadership, compliance, human resources, admissions and the smooth daily running of our services.

Our Commitment to Employees & Safeguarding

We aim to create a workplace where people feel respected, supported and encouraged to reach their potential. We promote equality, diversity and inclusion and are committed to fair recruitment and employment practices.

All appointments are subject to appropriate recruitment checks, references, right-to-work eligibility requirements and PVG (Protecting Vulnerable Groups) scheme membership or other safeguarding checks where applicable.
