Our Background

Who We Are

Divine Care Homes Scotland Ltd. is committed to delivering high-quality care and support to individuals who require assistance with their daily living, health and wellbeing. We recognise that choosing a care home is one of the most important decisions a person and their family may make. We aim to make that journey as reassuring and positive as possible.

Our approach is founded on the belief that every resident deserves individual attention, kindness, respect and the opportunity to live a fulfilling life.

We seek to provide more than accommodation and care. We aim to create welcoming communities where residents feel a sense of belonging, where friendships develop and where families feel confident that their loved ones are being cared for with compassion.

Purpose & Dedication

Our Mission

To deliver outstanding, person-centred care through compassion, professionalism, integrity and continuous improvement, ensuring that every resident experiences dignity, respect, comfort and the best possible quality of life.

Our Aspiration

Our Vision

To become a trusted and respected care provider in Scotland, recognised for excellence in care, strong leadership, dedicated people and a genuine commitment to improving lives.

Guiding Principles

Our Core Values: DIVINE

The acronym DIVINE represents the six core commitments that guide every care plan, everyday conversation, and leadership decision.

D

Dignity

We respect every individual's identity, choices, privacy and personal values at all times.

I

Individuality

We recognise that every person is unique, with their own life experiences, preferences and aspirations.

V

Value

We value our residents, families and employees and recognise the important contribution each makes to our care community.

I

Integrity

We act honestly, responsibly and transparently, always putting residents' interests first.

N

Nurturing

We create a caring and supportive environment where individuals feel safe, comfortable and valued.

E

Excellence

We strive for the highest standards through effective leadership, skilled professionals and continuous improvement.

Holistic Wellbeing

Our Philosophy of Care

We believe that good care goes beyond meeting physical needs. It involves understanding the person, respecting their choices, supporting their independence and promoting emotional, social and psychological wellbeing.

We take time to understand residents' life histories, cultural backgrounds, personal interests, routines and individual preferences.

We encourage residents to remain involved in decisions about their care and everyday lives, ensuring that their voices are heard and their wishes respected.

Compassionate Teams

Our People

Our employees are at the heart of everything we do. We seek to build a workforce of compassionate, skilled and dedicated professionals who share our commitment to quality care.

We invest in staff development, training, supervision and ongoing learning, creating a culture in which people feel supported, valued and motivated to deliver their best.

Trust & Partnership

Our Commitment to Residents and Families

We understand that placing your trust in a care provider is a significant decision. At Divine Care Homes Scotland Ltd., we aim to earn that trust through the quality of our care, the professionalism of our teams and the kindness shown in everyday interactions.

"Trust is built through actions, not words."
  • Listening to residents and respecting their wishes
  • Keeping families appropriately informed and involved
  • Protecting privacy, confidentiality and personal information
  • Respecting equality, diversity and individual beliefs
  • Supporting independence and personal choice
  • Responding to concerns openly and appropriately
  • Continuously reviewing and improving our services
